# 90-second pitch: Angry Sketches

Roles: **Speaker** talks, **Drawer** holds the paper, **Flinger** holds a phone. Have one fort pre-drawn (pigs in green!) and `display.html` open fullscreen, already in the lobby with both phones joined.

---

**0:00 – Hook (10 s)**
> "Every kid has drawn a castle on paper. We made it so your friends can knock it down."

**0:10 – Draw and scan (20 s)**. *Drawer holds up the pre-drawn fort, taps Scan fort.*
> "Black marker is wood, blue is ice, grey is stone, red is TNT, and green blobs are the pigs. One photo, and computer vision running right in the browser turns the drawing into a physics level in under a second."

*(The fort appears on the big screen.)*

**0:30 – Fling (25 s)**. *Flinger pulls back on the phone and lets go, then taps mid-flight.*
> "The other team's phones are the slingshot. Pull back, let go, and tap in mid-air for the bird's power. Every block has real physics: ice shatters, stone holds, TNT…"

*(Hit. Let the replay play.)*
> "…and here's the instant replay."

**0:55 – How (20 s)**
> "Retro Remix: pen and paper is the level editor. Under the hood, OpenCV in the browser finds the paper, flattens it and reads each colour. A Matter.js physics engine runs the game, and Socket.io turns any phone into a controller. No app to install, just scan a QR code. Three of us built it in parallel with Claude Code agents that talked to each other on a shared GitHub board."

**1:15 – Close (15 s)**
> "Then the teams swap, so you're always either building or destroying. Angry Sketches: draw it, scan it, smash it. Thank you!"

---

**If a scan fails on stage:** press **S** on the laptop (sample fort) and say *"and here's one we drew earlier"*. Keep going.
